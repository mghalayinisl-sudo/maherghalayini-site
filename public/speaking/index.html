Speaker bio

Dr. Maher Ghalayini is Vice President of Academic Affairs at SUNY Niagara County Community College, where he serves on the President's Cabinet and leads academic programs, faculty affairs, enrollment strategy, and accreditation, and works with the faculty bargaining unit. Before joining SUNY Niagara, he held senior academic leadership roles as Vice President, Senior Vice President, and Associate Vice President at St. Lawrence College, Georgian College, and Sheridan College, and served as a campus president with Corinthian Colleges.

Dr. Ghalayini has taught as an adjunct instructor since 2004 at institutions across Canada and the United States, including York University, Trent University, and the Insurance Institute of Canada. He holds an EdD in International Higher Education Administration from Northeastern University, where his doctoral research examined international student persistence, an LLM in Business Law from York University, and an MBA from Wilfrid Laurier University.
