Leadership

Senior academic roles at five institutions in Ontario and New York since 2005.

  1. 2025 – Present

    Vice President of Academic Affairs

    SUNY Niagara · Sanborn, NY

    Leading development of Future Forward 2026–2030, the College's strategic plan. Reorganized Academic Affairs and established an Academic Leadership Council. Oversaw 12 new credentials in health sciences, advanced manufacturing, and technology, expanded HyFlex and hybrid delivery to 60% of general education offerings, and raised more than $650,000 for bursaries, classrooms, and academic technology.

  2. 2023 – 2025

    Senior Vice President, Academic

    St. Lawrence College · Kingston, ON

    Directed academic strategy and restructured the academic division. Led development of 32 new credentials, renovated 88 classrooms for HyFlex delivery, and chaired the Strategic Enrolment Management executive through changing federal international policy. Raised funds for a $200K student bursary and a $450K paramedic lab.

  3. 2017 – 2023

    Associate Vice President, Academic

    Georgian College · Barrie, ON

    Previously Dean, Business, Management, and Automotive Business School of Canada

    Oversaw the University Partnership Centre, including joint delivery with Lakehead University and the Georgian@ILAC partnership. Launched the Centre for Credit Transfer and Prior Learning and led the CQAAP accreditation self-study and site visit.

  4. 2008 – 2017

    Associate Dean, Education, Allied Health, and Community Studies

    Sheridan College · Oakville, ON

    Previously Director, International

    As Director, International, grew international enrollment by 300% and built partnerships including the Chinese Academy of Fine Arts. As Associate Dean, led new program development, articulation agreements, and applied research.

  5. 2005 – 2008

    Campus President

    Corinthian Colleges · Toronto, ON

    Full leadership of academics, operations, enrollment growth, accreditation and regulatory compliance, and student services.
